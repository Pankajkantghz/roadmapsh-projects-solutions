Newton’s Laws of Motion

Fundamental principles describing the motion of objects

Newton’s First Law (Law of Inertia) +

An object remains at rest or continues to move with constant velocity unless acted upon by an external unbalanced force.

Newton’s Second Law of Motion +

The acceleration of an object is directly proportional to the net force acting on it and inversely proportional to its mass.

Mathematically: F = ma

Newton’s Third Law of Motion +

For every action, there is an equal and opposite reaction.

Real-life Examples of Newton’s Laws +

• Seatbelts protect passengers due to inertia.
• A heavier object needs more force to accelerate.
• Recoil of a gun is due to action–reaction.
